PERFORMANCE PROTOCOL // MARATHON EXECUTION

72-hour glycogen supercompensation, high-flux dual-transporter fueling (80–100g+/hr) & sub-threshold pacing discipline across 42.2 km.

T-72H → T-24H

72-Hr Glycogen Supercompensation

3-DAY LOAD
  • C
    Full 72-Hour Carb Load 10–12 g/kg/day

    Sustained across 3 days (72h → 24h out) to maximize intramuscular & hepatic glycogen stores. Strictly low fiber, low fat, moderate protein—use white rice, pasta, bagels, honey, and liquid carbs (maltodextrin/juice) to hit targets without GI fullness.

  • H
    Elevated Sodium & Fluid 12–18 mg/kg/day Na⁺

    Add 8–12 ml/kg sodium-rich electrolyte drinks (sodium citrate) daily across T-72h to T-12h. Expect +1–2 kg water/glycogen mass gain—this is fuel, not dead weight.

  • N
    Nitrate Loading Block (T-48h & T-24h) ~6–8 mg/kg NO₃⁻

    1× concentrated Beet It shot daily for 2 days prior to saturate muscle nitrite reserves and lower O₂ cost at marathon pace.

0K → 42.2K

High-Carb Fueling & Race Execution

CLOCKWORK
Exogenous Carb Engine 80–100g+ / hr (~1.2–1.5 g/kg/hr )

Execute 1 × 40g gel every 20–25 minutes on the clock from 20m onward using 1:0.8 maltodextrin:fructose dual-transporter formulas (SGLT1 + GLUT5 saturation) to spare liver/muscle glycogen.

Fluid & Sodium

400–800 ml/hr (6–11 ml/kg/hr ) + 700–1000 mg Na⁺/hr via electrolyte gels, salt caps, or course drink.

Caffeine Dose 2

Deploy 1.5–2.0 mg/kg caffeinated gel at 25–30 km to blunt late-stage central fatigue & RPE spike.

0–10K

Bank Patience: Strict sub-threshold control; never chase early surges. Take Gel #1 at 20–25m while gut blood flow is highest.

10–30K

Steady Rhythm & Clockwork Fueling: Cruise in aerobic flow, shelter in packs, hit every 20–25m gel & aid station; take Caffeine Dose 2 at 25–30K.

30–42.2K

The Race Begins: Hold posture, shorten ground contact, keep taking gels through 35K, and squeeze pace past fading runners from 35K to finish.

ASTRIDE Ergogenic Quick-Reference

MECHANISMS
Caffeine 3–6 mg/kg total

Adenosine receptor blockade; lowers RPE & boosts motor unit drive (single T-60m dose for 5K–HM; split T-15m + 25–30K for Marathon).

Dietary Nitrates (Beet It) 6–12 mg/kg • T-3h

Elevates nitric oxide bioavailability, reducing O₂ cost of submaximal & threshold running. Avoid antibacterial mouthwash.

Sodium Bicarbonate 0.25–0.3 g/kg • T-2.5h

Extracellular blood pH buffer against H⁺ acidosis—highest ROI in 5K & 10K (use enteric/hydrogel delivery).

Nomio (Isothiocyanates) 1 Shot • T-3h to T-2h

Broccoli sprout bioactives activating Nrf2 pathway; reduces lactate accumulation & oxidative stress across all 4 distances.

Sodium / Sodium Citrate 5–18 mg/kg

Expands plasma volume pre-race and maintains serum osmolality during HM/Marathon; sodium citrate minimizes GI osmotic distress.

PROTOCOL RULE: Nothing new on race day. Validate all carb rates, bicarb & caffeine splits in key race-specific workouts.
